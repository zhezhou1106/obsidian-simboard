## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Widgets: banner with greeting and live clock, month calendar linked to daily notes, quick links, a Markdown-backed todo list, and vault stats with an activity heatmap.
- The `simboard` code block chooses its widgets, their order and per-widget options.
- Todos: add, check off, delete and drag to reorder; finished items are cleaned up after a configurable number of days.
- Commands to open the dashboard note, insert a dashboard block and refresh open dashboards, plus an optional ribbon icon.
- Colors follow the active theme, with an optional high-contrast e-ink mode.
- Dashboard width slider that resizes open dashboards live.
- Settings are searchable from Obsidian's settings search (1.13+).
- Desktop only.
